## 4. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters — ~2,250 characters used)
```text
Intervals Companion is a fast, clean, and distraction-free mobile and tablet companion built for endurance athletes who plan and analyze their training on Intervals.icu.

Designed for quick daily checks before you head out the door and deep post-workout inspection after you finish, Intervals Companion brings your training calendar, structured workout steps, and activity telemetry into a native-feeling Android experience.

KEY FEATURES

• Weekly Training Calendar at a Glance
Browse your training week by week or tap any day to focus on today's schedule. Instantly see which workouts are completed and what sessions are coming up next, with smooth pull-to-refresh syncing and offline caching.

• Visual Planned Workout Diagrams & Step Breakdown
Inspect every scheduled workout before you train. View interactive step-by-step bar charts color-coded by intensity (Warmup, Active, Threshold, VO2max, and Recovery) with exact target pace, power, or heart-rate ranges and coach instructions.

• Detailed Completed Activity Telemetry & HR Zones
Dive into your finished runs, rides, swims, and strength sessions:
- Interactive time-series charts for Pace, Heart Rate, Cadence, and Elevation
- Athlete-accurate 7-zone Heart Rate distribution (Z1–Z7) synced directly from your personal Intervals.icu LTHR, resting HR, and max HR settings
- Automatic lap and interval split tables when structured intervals are recorded

• Multi-Sport & Status Filtering
Filter your feed by workout status (All, Incoming, or Done) and customize which sports appear on your calendar—supporting Running, Trail Running, Cycling, Gravel & MTB, Swimming, Rowing, Cross-Country Skiing, Strength, Crossfit/HIIT, Yoga, Pilates, and Walking/Hiking.

• Incoming Workouts Lookahead
Switch to the Incoming tab to preview your upcoming 7, 14, or 28 days of scheduled workouts in one clean chronological timeline.

• Daily Morning Workout Digest
Optionally enable a local morning notification at your preferred wake-up time so you know what workout is on your schedule for the day ahead—even when offline.

• Built for Phones & Tablets
Locked to a comfortable one-handed portrait layout on phones, with a dedicated two-pane split-screen workspace on tablets in landscape mode. Supports Dark, Light, and High-Contrast themes plus Metric (km, min/km) and Imperial (mi, min/mi) units.

PRIVACY FIRST & SECURE OAUTH 2.0
Sign in securely using official Intervals.icu OAuth 2.0 with PKCE. Your credentials are never shared with third-party servers, and your cached workouts are stored locally on your device.

Note: Intervals Companion is an independent, unofficial companion application created for the Intervals.icu athlete community and requires an existing Intervals.icu account.
```
